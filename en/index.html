      The method behind the cards

      Historical method

      After shuffling and cutting, every seventh card is kept. Matching ranks are read first, followed by seven stops around the significator. The same 12 cards are then dealt into four themed packets.

      Thesavros 770 · pp. 26–27

      Digital conventions

      The ends of the fan are adjacent. If the significator is absent, the Seven of Hearts takes priority over the Seven of Clubs. Each layout derives from the seismic fingerprint. The complete reading brings the recorded indications and their sources together.

      Algorithm and proof

      A historical and cultural recreation for entertainment. The readings do not predict actual events.
